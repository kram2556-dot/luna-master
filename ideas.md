# Luna Beauty Studio — Rebuild direction

## Chosen direction: Light Luxury Feminine
- Background: `#FFFBF7` only; no black surfaces.
- Ink: warm charcoal for readability, with dusty rose `#D4A5A5` as the signature accent.
- Typography: Tajawal 400 throughout. Body 16px, section titles 28px.
- Layout: every section is capped at 1200px and centered.
- Imagery: close-up makeup/bridal faces only; no phones, products, hands, or random objects.
- Motion: AOS fade-up for sections and images; subtle 1.05 image hover zoom.
- Conversion: clear WhatsApp CTA, floating WhatsApp pulse, booking form.
- Social proof: social icons in header and a prominent footer follow area.
